Leaves of Fate
January - March 2026

Over the course of several months, I worked with the Project Misfits team as a contractor to create assets for their game Leaves of Fate, a 2D platformer developed over the course of seven weeks.

Tools used: Godot, Adobe Animate

Character Animations: Az
I was the character artist and animator for Az, a companion character in the game. I was responsible for developing the final look of the character based on concept art, and creating her idle and walk animations.
Particle Effects: Death and Respawn
I developed the particle effect animations for the player character's death and respawn.
Particle Effects: Wind Gain and Drain Zones
I created tileable effects for wind gain and drain zones, areas that enhance or restrict the player character's movement.
Misc Particle Effects: Fire and Moveable Platform Highlights
I created two fire effects (hearth and breath) and a highlight effect for moveable platforms.
