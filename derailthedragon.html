Derail the Dragon

When a mighty drake was slain by the efforts of heroes, its soul remained, fueled by rage. Now it has possessed a man-made machine, twisting it into a new draconic body and bringing objects inside it to life with the wyrm's dark power. The locomotive is destroying anything that stands in its way, and it shrugs off all attacks from outside.

You, a gnome versed in earth magic, have managed to jump onto the very last car of the train. If you can just make it to the engine, you might be able to destroy the beast from inside.

Derail the Dragon - Artistic Game Development II project
March 17 2025-May 7 2025

A 7-person team project created over the course of seven weeks, I modeled, textured, rigged, and animated the enemies and environment. See the project website for more details, and my end of term showcase for more info on the work I created for the project. Tools used include ZBrush, Maya, Substance Painter, and Unreal Engine 5.
