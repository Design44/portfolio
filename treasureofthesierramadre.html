Treasure of the Sierra Madre

Digital Board Game

This game is in-progress - stay tuned for more info and a Steam release!

Welcome to the Sierra Madre! Whispers of gold lured you to the southwest, desperate for a chance to make quick riches. To come out on top, you're gonna need to use all the tools available to you to gather as much gold as possible; But to hold on to it, you're gonna need to be cautious and keep your wits about you. Working with your fellow man is paramount to navigate this rough desert safely — but can you really trust them? Be sure to make it back to the Cantina before time runs out — you don't wanna be left behind out here when the Federales ride in. Good luck prospector, you're gonna need it.

Treasure of the Sierra Madre
August 2025 - May 2026

A digital board game based on the classic novel Treasure of the Sierra Madre. Players must use cooperation and betrayal along with items and character abilities to obtain the most gold and make it back safely before the end of the game.

Created in Godot by a six person team plus two contractors, I served as 3D and lead technical artist, as well as a designer. Sponsored by Chupacabra Productions, who will be continuing development with a new team of students.

Tools used: Godot, Autodesk Maya, Material Maker
